# Claim 3 source audit

The audited source archive was retrieved 2026-07-30 and has SHA-256
`f1078eb9464172f9a941c1dd95354116ae749d64b91ec29947b47a588d2a1f9f`.

Theorem 2 is existential over a fixed one-layer log-precision nonlinear MLP RNN
and universal over valid sorted deterministic connectivity inputs. Proposition
2 supplies L-completeness under FO reductions. The depth corollary is
conditional: the paper does not prove an unconditional circuit lower bound.

The certificate uses a tuple-based layered reduction equivalent to the paper's
numeric `i+hn` construction. Tuple vertices make sortedness and FO definability
explicit and avoid relying on the prose claim that products are precomputed
from input size.

Primary L-completeness reference: Cook and McKenzie, *Problems complete for
deterministic logarithmic space*, 1987,
DOI `10.1016/0196-6774(87)90018-6`.
