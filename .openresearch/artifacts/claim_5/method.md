   initialization, and compare streams through 257 matrices to direct products.
2. Independently reconstruct DeltaNet's transvection and 694-step 9x9
   arithmetic program.

necessary lemma of the published DeltaNet proof. Route 4 invalidates the

the existential theorem. The result is therefore BLOCKED.
